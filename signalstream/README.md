# SignalStream — High-Intent Lead Intelligence

Premium B2B intelligence service delivering verified buying signals and trigger events to cybersecurity firms, MSPs, AI consultancies, commercial solar companies, and industrial services.

## Product Overview

SignalStream detects and verifies trigger events — funding rounds, hiring surges, compliance deadlines, breach indicators, technology adoption signals, and executive moves — then delivers enriched opportunities to your sales team weekly.
