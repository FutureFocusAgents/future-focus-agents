FAQ

Common questions

Is this a chatbot?
No. These are automated workflows and smart integrations that connect your existing tools — phone system, PMS, Google Business profile. Patients interact with familiar text messages and booking links, not chatbots. Everything runs behind the scenes.
Do I need to learn new software?
No. The automations run in the background and integrate with tools you already use. Your staff won't need any training. You'll get a simple dashboard to see what's happening, but the system works without any manual intervention.
How quickly will I see results?
Most practices see recovered calls and reduced no-shows within the first week. Review growth typically takes 30–60 days to compound. The $29/mo Starter plan typically pays for itself within the first 48 hours through recovered missed calls alone.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no lock-in periods. We believe the results will speak for themselves. Most practices see enough value in the first week that cancellation never crosses their mind.
Does this work with my practice management system?
We integrate with all major PMS platforms including Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and Curve. If you use something else, reach out and we'll confirm compatibility before you sign up.
How is this different from what my PMS already does?
Your PMS manages patient records. We automate the workflows between your PMS, phone system, Google, and patient communication. Think of it as the intelligent layer that connects everything and makes it work together automatically. They complement each other perfectly.
What exactly do you mean by "automation" and "smart systems"?
We build automated workflows that trigger based on real events in your practice. When a call is missed, a text goes out automatically. When an appointment is booked, reminders are scheduled. When a patient visits, a review request is sent. These are intelligent integrations between your existing tools — not chatbots, not AI gimmicks. Just reliable systems that do the work your staff shouldn't have to do manually.
